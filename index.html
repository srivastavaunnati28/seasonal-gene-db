What it catalogs

Four physiological systems, one seasonal switch.

Every gene in the database is mapped onto the pathway that transduces day length into physiology — from the pineal gland's melatonin clock to the reproductive axis it ultimately gates.

01

Photoperiod / Melatonin Pathway

AANAT, ASMT, MTNR1A, TSHB, DIO2, DIO3 — the retina-to-pineal circuit that turns night length into a hormonal signal.

02

Circadian Clock Core

CLOCK, BMAL1, PER1/2, CRY1/2 — the ~24h transcription-translation loop that sets the phase of the melatonin rhythm itself.

03

Seasonal Reproduction (HPG axis)

KISS1, GNRH1, RFRP3 — the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal circuitry that gates breeding season on and off.

04

Thyroid-Hormone Seasonal Switch

TSHB, DIO2, DIO3, EYA3 — the pars tuberalis feed-forward loop that converts melatonin duration into a binary LD/SD state.
