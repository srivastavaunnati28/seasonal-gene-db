Why it's different

Not a general gene database — a scoped one

Scope-gated search

Search only returns results for genes/pathways inside this database's defined photoperiod/seasonal-physiology scope. Unrelated queries return a clean "Not Found" instead of noise pulled from general databases.

HGNC / Ensembl / UniProt validated

Every gene symbol is checked against the official HGNC registry and resolved to stable identifiers — not just matched by free-text symbol.

Statistical rigor

p-values, sample sizes, and 95% confidence intervals are tracked per curated row, alongside a 4-tier evidence-grading system.

Moderated contributions

Community submissions require a verifiable PMID/DOI/GEO accession and are reviewed before appearing in search results.
